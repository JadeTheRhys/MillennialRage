Mental Health, But Make It Millennial

We joke about anxiety… because the alternative is crying in the shower before work.

We grew up with “stop crying or I’ll give you something to cry about.” Now we’re unlearning that mess one deep breath at a time.

Grounding Techniques

  • • Breath in for 4, hold for 2, out for 6
  • • Notice 5 things around you
  • • Relax shoulders you didn’t realize were clenched

Permission Slips

  • • You're allowed to rest
  • • You don’t have to earn kindness
  • • Your worth isn’t tied to productivity
